About / Amelia Cronan

Researcher.
Writer. Systems builder.

Mimi Corp is the working studio of Amelia Cronan—a social scientist, writer, and systems builder whose practice moves between political theory, ecological observation, public media, and practical infrastructure.

The common thread

I pay attention to the systems people inherit, the ones they build, and the places where those systems stop making sense.

My interests begin with land, governance, memory, and the ordinary structures people rely on. From there they branch into research, ecological record-keeping, audio conversation, websites, software, and public language.

Mimi Corp brings those forms together. The aim is not to make complicated work look simple. It is to make the real structure visible enough that people can understand it, trust it, and use it.
