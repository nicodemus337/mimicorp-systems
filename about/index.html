About mimi

research / ecology / media / systems

Amelia Cronan is a social scientist, writer, and systems builder whose work moves between political theory, ecological observation, public media, and practical infrastructure. mimicorp labs is the home base for that work: a place to gather research, field notes, publishing projects, web systems, and experiments in public language.

Her interests begin with land, governance, memory, and the ordinary systems people rely on. From there they branch into dissertation writing, articles, ecological record-keeping, audio conversation, and tools that help complicated work become easier to see and share.
