Short Story

discomfort is information.

My Feet Are Dirty is a modern children's story about sensory overwhelm, emotional self-awareness, and learning how to ask for help. Through Beau's experience, young readers see that discomfort is not bad behavior. It is information.

Designed with emotionally intelligent storytelling in mind, this book helps children build language around overstimulation, self-regulation, and communicating their needs with trusted adults.

Perfect For

sensitive kids and the adults who love them.

  • neurodivergent and sensory-sensitive children
  • parents navigating emotional regulation conversations
  • therapists, educators, and caregivers
  • families building healthier communication tools
