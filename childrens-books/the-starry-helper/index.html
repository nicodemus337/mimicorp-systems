Story

small lights help us feel brave again.

This gentle nighttime story gives children an image for asking for help when the world feels too big, too dark, or too much.

It is built around calm, courage, trusted companions, steady rituals, and the idea that hard moments do not have to be handled alone.

Perfect For

  • bedtime and transition routines

  • families practicing help-seeking language
  • caregivers building gentler hard-moment rituals
